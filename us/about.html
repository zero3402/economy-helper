Our Mission

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Do I need prior economics knowledge to use this site?

No prior knowledge is needed. Economy Helper is designed for everyone from beginners to experts. Our dictionary explains every term in plain language with real-world examples, and our columns break down complex concepts using everyday scenarios.

Q. When should I use the compound interest calculator?

Use it to project future returns on savings accounts, investment funds, or any compound-interest product. Enter your initial investment, monthly contributions, expected annual rate, and time horizon to see your wealth grow year by year.

Q. How accurate is the investment personality test?

The investment personality test is an educational tool designed for self-reflection and fun. Before making any real investment decisions, we strongly recommend consulting a certified financial advisor.

Q. How many columns are available?

We currently offer 51 in-depth economic columns covering topics such as inflation, interest rates, GDP, stagflation, and behavioral economics. New articles are added regularly.

Q. Is my data stored on your servers?

No. Economy Helper does not store any personal data on our servers. Your language preferences and test progress are saved only in your browser's local storage and can be cleared at any time.
