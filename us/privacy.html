1. Overview

Economy Helper ("economyhelper.com") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and improve our services

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our site. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your interests and prior visits.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to outside parties, except as required by law. Data processed through Google AdSense and Google Analytics is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

6. Data Security

Our site uses HTTPS to securely transmit data and we implement reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • You can refuse cookies by adjusting your browser settings.
  • You can delete local storage data directly through your browser.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
